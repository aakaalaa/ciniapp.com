## Who we are

Cini is a movie and TV recommendation app for iPhone, provided by Abimbola Akala, doing business as Cini ("Cini", "we", "us"). This policy covers the Cini app and the website at ciniapp.com.

If you have a question about this policy or your data, email [hello@ciniapp.com](mailto:hello@ciniapp.com).

## What we collect

### Information you give us

| What | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in details | Your name, email address and profile photo from Apple or Google. If you use Hide My Email, we get Apple's relay address instead. | To create and secure your account |
| Phone number | The number you verify with a one-time text code | To verify your account |
| Profile | Username and date of birth | To set up your profile and confirm you're old enough to use Cini |
| Taste and preferences | Titles you've seen, your streaming services, favorite genres, and the kinds of stories you like | To personalize your picks |
| Activity in the app | Moods you pick, titles you save, mark as seen, skip, rate or share, and searches you run | To recommend titles and improve your picks over time |
| Messages to us | Anything you send to hello@ciniapp.com | To answer you |
| Waitlist sign-ups | Your email, if you join the waitlist on ciniapp.com | To tell you when Cini launches |

### Information created as you use Cini

- **Your taste profile:** a set of numbers that summarizes what you like, built from your preferences and activity. It's how Cini gets better at picking for you.
- **Cini Points and credits:** your in-app balances.
- **Account dates:** when you joined and when you last used the app.
- **Device token:** if you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your phone.

Like any app that connects to the internet, the services we use receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, device type and iOS version, so they can deliver the app to you.

### What we don't collect

We don't collect your contacts, photos, precise location, health data or payment card details. We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## How we use your information

- To run your account and keep it secure
- To recommend movies and shows that match your mood and taste, and to improve those picks as you use Cini
- To show your watchlist, history, ratings, Cini Points and credits on all your devices
- To send notifications, if you turn them on
- To answer your questions and fix problems
- To prevent abuse and keep Cini safe
- To understand, in totals that don't identify you, how Cini is used so we can make it better
- To follow the law

We don't use your information to show you ads, and we don't sell it.

## Who we share it with

We share your information only as described here.

- **Service providers who run Cini for us.** They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  - **Google Firebase and Google Cloud** host your account, store your data, run our backend and recommendation service, send sign-up text codes and deliver notifications.
  - **Typesense** powers search. It receives what you type into search.
- **Apple and Google**, when you sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to that sign-in.
- **TMDB**, which hosts the posters and images you see in the app. Your device loads them from TMDB's servers, so TMDB sees your IP address.
- **People you choose to share with.** When you share a title, it goes to whoever you send it to.
- **If the law requires it**, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Cini.
- **If Cini is sold or merged.** Your information may transfer to the new owner, who must respect this policy.

The service that picks your recommendations receives your moods and your taste profile, not your name, email or phone number.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your information within 30 days. Copies in backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, and we may keep a limited amount for longer when the law requires it or to prevent fraud.

Waitlist emails are kept until Cini launches and we've emailed you, or until you ask us to remove yours.

## Your choices and rights

- **See or copy your data.** Ask us for a copy at [hello@ciniapp.com](mailto:hello@ciniapp.com).
- **Correct it.** Ask us, and we'll fix anything that's wrong.
- **Delete it.** See [Delete your account](/delete-account).
- **Notifications.** Turn them on or off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as the right to object to or limit how we use your information, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to use any of these rights. We'll answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking.

**California residents:** we don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

**If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK:** we use your information to provide the service you signed up for, for our legitimate interests in keeping Cini secure and improving it, to meet legal obligations, and with your consent for notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time.

## Children

You must be 13 or older to use Cini, and we check your date of birth when you sign up. If we learn that someone under 13 has created an account, we'll delete it. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us.

## Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and stored with Google Cloud, which encrypts it at rest. Only the people who build and run Cini can access it, and only when they need to. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## Where your data is stored

Cini and its service providers store and process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. If you live outside the US, your information will be transferred there, with the protections this policy describes.

## Our website

ciniapp.com doesn't use cookies, ads or analytics, and it loads nothing from other companies. If you join the waitlist, your email is stored in a Google Sheet we control. The site is hosted by GitHub, which may keep server logs such as IP addresses for security.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

## Contact us

Email [hello@ciniapp.com](mailto:hello@ciniapp.com) with any question about your privacy.
